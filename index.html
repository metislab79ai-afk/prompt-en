FROM QUESTION TO ACTION

Your research journey

  1. Choose your country

    Select a prompt language. The workshop interface stays in English.

  2. Explore a challenge

    Choose a learning scenario or describe your own. Scenarios are hypotheses, not verified national diagnoses.

  3. Add your perspective

    Define your learners, desired change, and constraints. Write in your preferred language.

  4. Research with Gemini

    Copy your prompt, open Gemini, select Deep Research if available, paste, and review its research plan before starting.

After research: check the sources, question the assumptions, and choose one action your institution can test.
